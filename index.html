Core ideas

What chemoinformatics does

⌬

Representation

Molecules become machine-readable as line notations (SMILES, InChI), connection tables and molecular graphs.

⚖

Descriptors

Numbers that summarise a structure: molecular weight, logP, hydrogen-bond donors, polar surface area and thousands more.

⧉

Similarity search

Binary fingerprints and the Tanimoto coefficient find close analogues among millions of compounds in seconds.

◎

QSAR & ML

Models link structure to activity, toxicity or solubility so chemists can prioritise which compounds to make.
